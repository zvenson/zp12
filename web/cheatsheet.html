Sound

Channelsa pad plays on the channel of its position: pad 3 of every bank on channel 3 (fader 3, mute 3, solo 3). One sound at a time each: a new hit cuts the last (the hats share 3). Pads 1–2 have the filter
1–2a 4-pole resonant low-pass: open as CUT at the hit, closing two octaves as the sound decays (RESO)
3–6 · 7–8a fixed low-pass · no filter (OUT: CUT / RESO --)
45 → 33TRUNC, SPEED: played at 33/45 of its rate: lower, grainier. Own samples also at ×2 (the editor): an octave down on the pad

Make a beat

  1. REC (ARMED), PLAY: COUNT 4 3 2 1, then it records.
  2. Play kick and snare. AUTO CORRECT puts them on the grid.
  3. REC again at the end: an empty loop (BARS AUTO) is now as long as your take.
  4. Hats step by step: hit the hat, hold SEQ: the white keys are its 16 steps (lit = a hit), tap to set / clear; OCT+ / OCT− the next / last bar.
  5. REC while it plays: overdub on top (DUB BAR: from the next 1); REC again: off.
  6. Turn TUNE, FINE, DECAY (EDIT → SOUND) or CUT while recording: the pad's hits keep what you turned (a red dot in the step grid).
  7. A chord: white 8 (A8), or OCT+, white 8 (C8). Melodic: ARP (MULTI PITCH).
  8. Another loop: a black key, record into it. Songs: SEQ → SONG.

Effects in 3 steps

  1. Hit the pad (the snare).
  2. EDIT until the LCD says SENDS.
  3. KNOB 4 = REV, KNOB 3 = DLY. Shape them: FX.

Your start: it plays A5, C1, A1 + A6: put your own sounds there. In the browser (Chrome / Edge, USB): zp12.designburgapps.com/editor samples (mic, interface, line-in), drops your own sounds onto the pads or chops a loop to pads (one chop a pad), /backup saves and restores everything. No answer? Hold OCT− while switching on (USB rescue).

Recording

Count-inGLO → CLICK → COUNT: OFF (it records at once), 1 bar (4 3 2 1), 2 bars (8 … 1)
AUTOa loop with BARS AUTO takes the length of its first take, in whole bars (REC up to a quarter past a bar line: that bar)
QUANTAUTO CORRECT to OFF, 1/8, 1/8T, 1/16, 1/16T, 1/32, 1/32T
SWING50, 54, 58, 63, 67, 71 %: the off 1/16s (with QUANT 1/8: the off 1/8s) come later
Overduba hit where one is: the louder stays. Up to 512 hits a loop
Velocitythe keys play at one level; USB MIDI velocity is played and recorded (8 steps)
Per hitTUNE, FINE, DECAY, CUT turned while recording (within 0.6 s of the turn) stay with the pad's hits
Step grid16 steps of 1/16 a bar; a step set by hand plays at a fixed level. A hit off the 1/16 grid shows, but a tap adds one beside it. In an AUTO loop the first step makes it 1 bar
EraseLFO + a pad: playing, its hits go as the playhead passes; stopped, all at once

Saving and safety

Savedby itself when stopped and 3 s after a change; SAVE now (playing: SAVES AT STOP)
Not savedmute / solo and the DJ filter (FX → FILTER): gone at power-off
UndoEDIT held + OCT−: the last clear, copy or stopped erase of a loop, one step. Not an erase while playing, not WAVE → COPY
RESETGLO → SETUP: every pad, mix, effect back to the factory, all loops and songs gone. Back up first
Backupzp12.designburgapps.com/backup: everything to a file and back (Chrome / Edge, USB)
Rescueno answer? Hold OCT− while switching on: the USB rescue, then install again
DJ filtersounds dull or thin? FX → FILTER may be on (centre = OFF)
