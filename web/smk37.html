The Firmware Hub · M-VAVE SMK-37 Pro & Elite

Open firmware for the SMK-37

The 37-key controller with pads, knobs, faders and wheels, turned into a synth, a groovebox, a drone machine or a chord harp: open-source firmwares, made for it or ported from the FM-1, each installed from its maker's installer.

Pick your keyboard's own model in the installer. Most firmwares here come as three packages: SMK-37 Pro, SMK-37 Elite and MKE-P37 (a Starrykey 37 Play takes the Pro's). They install in Chrome or Edge over USB from their makers' installers. Back up your sounds first; M-Vave's updater takes the keyboard back to its factory firmware, and holding OCT− while switching on is the rescue.
