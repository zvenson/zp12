How to play

  • White keys 1–16 play the pads of banks A and B (OCT +: C and D, a piano and chords; OCT −: back). The pad names are printed on the keys.
  • Black keys are the loops 1–11. PLAY runs the loop, REC arms recording (PLAY counts in), REC again while playing overdubs. REC held 2 s clears the loop.
  • KNOB 1–4 are the faders of channels 1–4 (SEL: 5–8). SELECT sets the tempo, ENV taps it.
  • EDIT: the last pad's sample, tune, decay, truncation, filter, sends. FX: filter, chorus, delay, reverb. SEQ tapped: loop length, quantize, swing, songs; SEQ held: the 16 steps on the white keys.
  • Hold FX + white keys 1–4: roll, roll, reverse, tape stop. Hold GLO + white keys: mute / solo. Hold LFO + a pad: erase its hits. ARP: the last pad over all 27 keys.
  • SAVE keeps everything (it also saves by itself when stopped), here in this browser.

Hold a button with the mouse and play the computer keys at the same time; on a phone, use two fingers. All of it: the cheat sheet.

Computer keyboard

The letters are printed on the keys above. Two rows, like a piano:

Keys C4 .. E5Z S X D C V G B H N J M , L . ; /
Keys F3 .. B3Q 2 W 3 E 4 R
Keys F5 .. G5I 9 O
Buttons1 HOME, 5 ENV, 7 LFO, 8 ARP, 0 SEQ, - EDIT, = GLO, [ FX, ] SAVE, Enter SEL, Space PLAY, \ REC, ← → OCT − / +
Knobs↑ ↓ PRESETS (the pad's sample); drag a knob up / down or scroll on it

The positions are those of a US keyboard; on other layouts the same physical keys play.

MIDI

A MIDI keyboard or pad controller plays it as over USB on the device: notes 36–67 the pads A1–D8, Program Change 0–15 the loops 1–16.

Web MIDI works in Chrome, Edge, Opera and Firefox; not in Safari.

Your loops

Loops, sounds, songs and settings stay in this browser (localStorage), as the FM-1 keeps them in its flash.

Not in the simulator: USB. Your own samples (the sample editor), backup and the installer need a real FM-1 on a cable; here you play the factory kit.
