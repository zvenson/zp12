Free firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

A 12-bit sampling drum machine in your hands.

32 pads at 26.04 kHz, pitched the old way: no interpolation, the aliasing is the sound. Eight channels with their filters, eleven loops on the black keys, songs, step editing, swing, and the reverb and delay of sloopDX. Install it from the browser; going back is one click.

Highlights

The crunch

12-bit samples at 26.04 kHz, pitched by their playback rate with nothing smoothed: tuned up they skip, down they repeat. TUNE in semitones and cents, 45 → 33 for the grain.

Eight channels

Every pad plays on a channel, one sound at a time: a new hit cuts the last. Channels 1–2 have a resonant 4-pole low-pass that follows the decay, 3–6 a fixed one, 7–8 none.

Faders on the knobs

KNOB 1–4 are the channel faders, SEL held for 5–8. The LCD's four columns always say what the knobs do now; a page lets go of them by itself.

Eleven loops

The black keys are loops: stopped they switch at once, playing they wait for the end of the loop. Each its own length, 1–32 bars, or AUTO from your first take.

Record and step

Real time with a count-in and AUTO CORRECT from 1/8 to 1/32T, overdub, erase while it plays. Hold SEQ: the white keys are the 16 steps of a pad.

Swing in steps

50, 54, 58, 63, 67, 71 %: the classic steps on the 16ths (or the 8ths). Tap tempo on ENV, the click on REC only or always.

Songs

Four songs, each a chain of loops with repeats. The LCD shows where you are: S2.03 L4, song two, step three, loop four.

Reverb and delay

sloopDX's stereo chorus, tempo delay and reverb with pre-delay. Each pad sends to them, and has a drive of its own.

Kept in flash

Sounds, mix, effects, loops and songs save by themselves when you stop, in a room of the flash sloopDX leaves free: your sloopDX banks stay where they are.

Make a beat

  1. REC then PLAY: a bar of count-in.
  2. Play kick and snare on the white keys. AUTO CORRECT puts them on the 16ths.
  3. REC again at the end: the loop is as long as your take.
  4. Hit the hat, hold SEQ: the white keys are its 16 steps. Tap to set them, OCT+ for the next bar.
  5. SEQ tapped, KNOB 4: swing to 63 %.
  6. OCT+, white key 9: a sampled E-piano chord. ARP: a bass over all 27 keys.
  7. REC on, turn TUNE or DECAY: the hits keep what you turned, a pitch sweep stays in the loop.
  8. FX: the DJ filter on the whole mix, low-pass to the left, high-pass to the right.
  9. A black key: the next loop. Songs: SEQ → SONG. SAVE held + a black key: the loop saved there; REC held: cleared.

The sounds

Bank A and B a full kit, C bank A lower and grainier at 45 → 33 and a grand piano (two funky stabs, Cm9 and F13, and a note to play over all keys), D what a sampler is for: chords and phrases. Put any of the 27 on any pad (hit it, turn PRESETS); your own samples come with the web editor.

Under the hood

Samples12-bit linear, two in three bytes, at 26.04 kHz or 27.5 kHz
Pitchthe playback rate, zero-order hold: no interpolation, the aliasing kept
Channels 1–24-pole resonant low-pass, its cutoff following the decay
Channels 3–6 · 7–8a fixed low-pass · none
Sequencer96 ticks a quarter, 16 loops of 1–32 bars, 512 hits each, 4 songs
Effectsstereo chorus, tempo delay, FDN reverb with pre-delay (from sloopDX), a drive per pad
Memoryeverything saved in 0xC4000–0xD7FFF, two copies; sloopDX's data untouched
Own samplescoming: a web editor that converts your WAVs to 12 bit, bit for bit as the FM-1 plays them. Recording into the FM-1 itself is not possible: it has no audio input.

Install

  1. Connect the FM-1 by USB, open the installer in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Press Install. About a minute; the FM-1 restarts twice. The splash shows the version.
  3. Back to sloopDX or the stock firmware any time with their installers. No answer? Hold OCT− while switching on: the USB rescue.

zp12 is an early test build: try it, and tell what you find on GitHub. Unofficial firmware, free and without warranty: you install it at your own risk.

Questions

Can I record samples into the FM-1?

No: the FM-1 has no audio input, and its USB audio only goes to the computer. Your own sounds will come from the web editor: drop a WAV, trim it, send it.

Does it replace sloopDX?

While it is installed, yes; your sloopDX projects, DX7 banks and kit stay in their own part of the flash, and the sloopDX installer puts sloopDX back.

Is it a copy of a vintage sampler?

No. It is inspired by how the 12-bit samplers of the 80s sound and work; it ships none of their sounds, code or names. Those names are trademarks of their owners.

Where are the chords?

Press OCT+ (the LEDs C and D light), then white key 9: D1, the E-piano chord. ARP plays it over all keys.

How do I start over?

GLO, KNOB 4 (RESET) twice to the right: sounds, mix, effects, loops and songs as new.
