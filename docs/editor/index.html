Your own samples

Drop a sound, trim it, hear it as the FM-1 will play it (12 bit, 26 kHz, no interpolation), and put it on a pad. Or sample it right here, from a microphone, an audio interface or the line-in. Your samples then turn up on the FM-1 too: hit a pad, turn PRESETS. Chrome or Edge, over USB.

Your sounds never leave your computer: everything happens in this page, and they go over the USB cable to the FM-1 only. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

4 · Add a sample

Or sample it: a microphone, an audio interface, the line-in (on a phone: its microphone). As on the old samplers, it can start by itself when the sound comes in (above the threshold), and it stops with a click or when the room is full.

Space: play, then tap along (each tap a marker; space while stopped plays) · click a chop: hear it as the FM-1 plays it · double-click: a marker · drag: move it · ← →: nudge · Del: remove · K: keep / leave out · 1–9, 0: hear a chop · wheel: zoom · the handle at the bottom: the chop's length

Equal parts and the grid cut the part between the first and the last marker (with fewer than two: all of it); the last marker then ends the last chop.

Each pad plays on its own channel (pad 1–8 = channel 1–8 in every bank): 1–2 the filter (CUT, RESO) 3–6 a fixed low-pass (9 / 12 kHz) 7–8 open. Two pads on one channel cut each other (A1 and B1).

Good to know

• Sampling here: the browser asks once for the microphone; pick the input (an interface, the line-in) after that. Echo cancelling, noise suppression and automatic gain are switched off, so it sounds as it comes in. Turn the source up until the meter nearly reaches the right end; Normalise does the rest.

• Room: 96 KB free = about 2.5 s at 26 kHz, 3.4 s at 45 → 33, 5 s at ×2, 6.8 s at ×2.7 (with sloopDX's bank room 2.5 times that). Up to 24 samples.

• Stored fast, played slow is the old samplers' trick and much of their sound: at ×2 the sample takes half the room, the pad plays it an octave down (TUNE −12, set for you), with half the bandwidth and twice the grit. Breaks and chords love it; a hat not so much.

• Chop to pads: load or sample a loop, then Chop to pads. Tap along (space), find the hits, or cut it in equal parts or on a tempo grid; drag the markers, leave chops out, shorten them. Every chop goes onto its own pad, from the pad you choose on (16 at most), all in one go. A pad's channel is its position (1–8 in every bank): chops on pads 1–2 get the filter (CUT, RESO), 3–6 a fixed low-pass, 7–8 none, and A1 and B1 cut each other as the original's channels do. Stopped while writing, the FM-1 stays as it was. Each chop takes one of the 24 places.

• Your samples are in the backup. Replacing one: delete it, add the new one.

• Private: the sounds are read and converted in your browser and sent over USB to the FM-1; nothing goes to our server (the page only counts its visits, without cookies).

• Trimmed inside the sound, the ends are faded (1 ms in, 2 ms out): no click where you cut.

• On the FM-1 they come after the 27 factory samples: hit a pad, turn PRESETS, or EDIT → WAVE.

• Your own start: switched on, the FM-1 plays its ba-dum-tss on A8, A7, A1 and B1. Put your sounds there and it greets you with them.
