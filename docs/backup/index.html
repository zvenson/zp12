Back up zp12

Everything on your FM-1 in one file: loops, songs, sounds, mix, effects and your samples, and if you like sloopDX's DX7 user banks and MY KIT too. Put it back whenever you want. Chrome or Edge, over USB.

1 · Connect

Plug in the FM-1 with zp12 1.3 or newer running, close other music apps and editors that use MIDI, then connect. The browser asks for MIDI access with SysEx: allow it.

NOT CONNECTED

2 · Download a backup

3 · Restore

Choose a backup file. What it holds is written back exactly as it was, then the FM-1 restarts. What is on the FM-1 in those parts now is replaced: download a backup of it first if you may want it.

Good to know

  • Stop playing first: the backup stops the sequencer, and the sound may click while the flash is written.
  • Nothing else of sloopDX is in a backup or touched by a restore: its projects, presets and settings stay as they are.
  • A backup of sloopDX's banks can be put back while zp12 runs, before you switch to sloopDX.
  • The file is checked (CRC) before anything is written; a damaged file is refused.
  • Private: the backup is read over USB and saved on your computer; nothing of it goes to our server.
  • Please always check the latest firmware version: zp12.designburgapps.com/install.
